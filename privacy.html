Privacy

How we handle
your information.

Short version: we collect what you send us, we use it to decide whether to back you or reply to you, we treat your pitch as confidential, and we don't track you around the internet.

Last updated · 8 October 2026 Version · 2026-10-08

Who we are

Tirla Ventures is an early-stage investment firm based in Bengaluru, India. We are currently completing our entity registration. Once that is done we'll publish the registered name and address here, and this page will be updated.

Until then, reach us at hello@tirla.vc for anything on this page.

What we collect

Only what you choose to give us. If you fill in the Tirla Open application, that's:

  • About you — name, email, LinkedIn, where you're building from, and who you're building with
  • About the company — its name and website, what you're building, what exists today, and a link to it
  • Your thinking — your insight, why now, where this goes, the evidence behind it, and what you've learned recently
  • About the round — your stage, whether you've raised before, how much you're raising, and what it buys you
  • Anything else you choose to tell us

We also record the moment you ticked the consent box, and which version of this page you agreed to, so that both of us have a clear record of what you said yes to.

If you email us directly about investing, the Tirla Circle, or Tirla Signals, we have whatever you put in that email.

A note on drafts. While you're filling in the application, your answers are saved in your own browser so you don't lose them if the tab closes. That draft stays on your device and is never sent to us. It's cleared the moment you submit, and you can clear it yourself any time by clearing your browser data.

Why we collect it

  • To read and evaluate your application
  • To reply to you, and to keep talking if we're interested
  • To answer investor enquiries about the Tirla Circle
  • To send Tirla Signals, our newsletter — only if you've asked for it, and you can stop any time

We don't use your details for anything else, and we don't sell them or share them for advertising. Ever.

Your pitch stays confidential

This isn't something the law requires us to say. We're saying it because it's the thing founders care about most.

We treat what you send us as confidential. We read it inside the investment team and we don't pass it outside that team — not to other founders, not to our network, not to another investor — without asking you first.

Like most venture firms, we don't sign NDAs at first contact. That's not a trick; it's because we see a lot of adjacent ideas and can't responsibly promise otherwise. So send us enough to make the opportunity obvious, and hold back the part that only matters once we're actually in a conversation. If we go deeper together, we're glad to sign something then.

Who else sees it

As few people as possible. We use three kinds of service, and that's the whole list:

  • Our database provider stores your application. We've chosen to keep that database on servers in India. The provider is a company registered outside India, so its staff could in principle access it for support reasons.
  • Our email provider stores our inbox, including any mail you send us and any alert telling us a new application arrived. Their servers may be outside India.
  • Our website host keeps the standard server logs every web server writes — IP address, browser, pages visited. We don't read these for anything except keeping the site up.

Nobody else. We don't use advertising networks, data brokers or lead-generation tools, and we never sell your details. If we add another tool later, we'll name it here before we start using it.

How long we keep it

If we don't take things forward, we delete your application and the thread around it within 24 months. If we do invest, or we're in an active conversation, we keep it for as long as that relationship runs and as long as the law requires afterwards.

You can ask us to delete it sooner. See below.

Your rights

You can ask us to:

  • Show you what we hold about you
  • Correct anything that's wrong or out of date
  • Delete what we hold
  • Withdraw your consent — and withdrawing is as easy as giving it was. One line by email is enough, and you don't have to explain why.

When you withdraw consent, we delete what we hold unless the law requires us to keep it. We'll do our best to come back to you within a few working days.

Email hello@tirla.vc with "Privacy" in the subject line.

Questions and complaints

If something about your data bothers you, write to hello@tirla.vc and a member of the team will handle it personally. We'd much rather hear from you directly first.

If we don't resolve it to your satisfaction, you can complain to the Data Protection Board of India under the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023.

Keeping it safe

We're a small team and we keep the surface small on purpose. Applications travel over an encrypted connection and are stored encrypted. The database is configured so that the website can only add an application — the key published in this site's code cannot read a single existing record back. Reading applications requires a separate administrator login, held by the investment team, protected by strong unique passwords and two-factor authentication.

No one can promise perfect security. If a breach does affect your data, we'll notify the Data Protection Board without delay and file the detailed report required within 72 hours — and we'll tell you directly too.

Cookies and analytics

We don't use any. This site sets no cookies, runs no analytics, and loads nothing from third-party servers — the fonts and graphics are built into the page itself. Nobody is counting you, and no advertising network learns that you were here.

If we ever add analytics, we'll say so on this page first.

Children

This site is meant for founders, investors and other adults doing business with us. It isn't intended for anyone under 18, and we don't knowingly collect information from children. If you believe a child has sent us something, write to us and we'll delete it.

Changes to this page

We'll update this page as Tirla grows — when our entity registration completes, and if we start using new tools. The date at the top always shows the current version. For anything significant, we'll email the people it affects.
